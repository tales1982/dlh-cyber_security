PURPOSE
-------

This inventory is the authoritative mapping of MedDefense assets to
the data they store or have privileged access to.  It is the reference
document used for:

  -  Risk assessment and asset prioritization (Module 1 risk register)
  -  Hardening scope decisions (Module 2 baseline)
  -  Detection-rule prioritization (Module 3 SIEM)
  -  Incident-response impact analysis (Modules 4 and 5)
  -  HIPAA breach notification scoping when an asset is compromised

The inventory does NOT enumerate workstations one by one (workstation
data is generally local-only and ephemeral).  Workstations are
classified by department because their privileges and the data their
users handle differ by role.


DATA SENSITIVITY CLASSIFICATION
-------------------------------

  CRITICAL    Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA.
              Exposure triggers mandatory breach notification under
              45 CFR Part 164 Subpart D when affecting > 500
              individuals, or HHS Wall of Shame at any volume after
              risk assessment.

  HIGH        Personally Identifiable Information (PII), financial
              data subject to PCI-DSS or contract obligations, or
              authentication material whose exposure would enable
              compromise of CRITICAL assets.

  MEDIUM      Internal operational data, business documents, or
              reference data whose exposure would harm the
              organization but does not trigger regulatory
              notification.

  LOW         Public-facing or transient data, default workstation
              caches, no regulated content under MedDefense data
              handling policy.

CRITICAL ACCESS PATHS (HIGH-RISK PATHWAYS)
==========================================

The following access paths are operationally normal but represent
the highest-impact compromise routes.  Detection rules should
prioritize anomalies on these paths.

  PATH 1   svc_healthsync  =>  SRV-HEALTH-DB
           Normal source:  SRV-HEALTH-DB itself (Type 5 service logon)
                           or backup pulls from SRV-BACKUP-01
           Anomalous:      Any other source.  This path is the
                           direct route from a stolen credential to
                           PHI exposure.  Watched in 4x04 hunt.

  PATH 2   robert.kim  =>  any server in 10.10.20.0/24
           Normal source:  WS-ADMIN-01 (per maintenance schedule)
           Anomalous:      Any other source, any non-business hour
                           timing.

  PATH 3   svc_backup  =>  any production server
           Normal:         SRV-BACKUP-01 -> production server during
                           backup window
           Anomalous:      Reverse direction, or off-window timing


PHI EXPOSURE SUMMARY
====================

If the HEALTHBANE attacker is confirmed to have read patient or claims
data, the affected datasets are:

  PRIMARY EXPOSURE
    Source:    SRV-HEALTH-DB / health_records.patients
    Records:   ~ 47 000 patient identities
    Field set: name, DOB, SSN (encrypted), address, phone, diagnoses,
               medications, lab results, clinical notes
    Trigger:   HIPAA breach notification within 60 calendar days
               of discovery if access is confirmed and risk
               assessment fails to demonstrate low probability of
               compromise (45 CFR 164.402)

  SECONDARY EXPOSURE  (overlapping cohort)
    Source:    SRV-INS-DB / claims.members
    Records:   ~ 51 000 member identities
    Field set: name, DOB, address, policy number, claim diagnoses
    Trigger:   Same HIPAA framework, plus state PII statutes if
               members include MedDefense employees

  TERTIARY EXPOSURE
    Source:    SRV-FILE-01 / \shared\imaging
    Records:   ~ 8 400 patients
    Field set: imaging studies linked to patient ID

The combined notification cohort if all three sources are confirmed
exposed would be approximately 50 000 - 55 000 individuals (deduped
across the three sources).
