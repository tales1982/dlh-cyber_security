C2 PROTOCOL FINGERPRINT
=======================
Channel:        TLS 1.2 with RSA key exchange (no PFS)
Server cipher:  ECDHE-RSA-AES128-GCM-SHA256
SNI:            sync.healthbane-c2.net  (consistent across all
                289 beacons)
ALPN:           h2 (HTTP/2)
JA3 hash:       72a589da586844d7f0818ce684948eea
JA3S hash:      35dd7a8965c100f7be58d8df9f5d9d6d
Beacon URI:     POST /api/v1/checkin?id=<UUID-static>&n=<seq>
Beacon body:    JSON wrapped in RC4 with hardcoded key
                (key recovered in 4x03 RAT triage:
                K = "h$lthb4n3-2026-Q2!!"
                yes, the attacker baked the campaign name into
                the key.  Stage 1 of operational discipline:
                lacking)
Heartbeat:      300 +/- 10 seconds
Inbound size:   typically 96 bytes (acks); occasional 1.4KB
                packets when commands are issued (none observed
                in this 48h window)


DNS EXFILTRATION INDICATORS
===========================
The 48h PCAP window contains TWO DNS queries for
data-sync.healthbane-c2.net:

  [T-150]  2026-04-15T13:42:11Z   TXT query, resolved 185.220.101.46
  [T-151]  2026-04-15T13:42:14Z   TXT query, resolved 185.220.101.46

Both queries are 39-byte payloads.  4x03 will identify these as
exfiltrator code-path test pings (the exfiltrator script ran a
single beacon to verify reachability of the DNS exfil channel
during deployment).  No high-volume DNS exfil was observed in
4x01 because the attacker had not yet collected and staged data.


LATERAL MOVEMENT TRACES (LIMITED)
=================================
The 4x01 PCAP captured east-west traffic for VLAN-3 only (records
department).  Cross-VLAN traffic to VLAN-20 (server segment) was
NOT captured because the access-layer switch SPAN port for VLAN-3
does not see traffic that is routed through the L3 core to VLAN-20.

The 4x01 evidence shows ZERO lateral movement during the 48h
window.  This was reported as "no lateral movement observed" but
NOT as "no lateral movement occurred" -- the difference matters
because 4x04 later showed that lateral movement DID occur on
2026-05-05+, well after the 4x01 collection window ended.

The 4x01 report flagged this as a critical collection gap.

NOTES FOR 4x05 RECONSTRUCTION
-----------------------------
  - 4x01 is authoritative for C2 establishment timing and protocol
    fingerprint.  The 5-minute beacon and the JA3 hash are the
    most reliable network IOCs in the entire campaign.
  - 4x01 has a 14-day visibility gap (Apr 16 - Apr 30) that is
    only filled by IR firewall session logs.  Cross-reference any
    network claim about that period against IR-FW data.
  - The two DNS exfil test pings (T-150, T-151) prove the
    exfiltration capability was deployed early but do NOT prove
    actual exfiltration of data.
  - The 4-second clock skew between PCAP capture timestamps and
    perimeter firewall session log timestamps was characterized
    in this investigation: PCAP timestamps are roughly 4s ahead
    of firewall (PCAP captured downstream of the firewall, with
    the kernel adding the timestamp at packet receipt; firewall
    timestamps the SYN at policy decision time).  Use the firewall
    timestamp as authoritative for connection initiation.
