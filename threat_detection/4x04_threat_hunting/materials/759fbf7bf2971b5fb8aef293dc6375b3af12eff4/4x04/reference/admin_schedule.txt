WHAT ROBERT KIM DOES NOT DO
---------------------------

The following activities are NEVER part of Robert Kim's documented
maintenance schedule.  If any of these appear in SIEM data attributed
to robert.kim or originating from WS-ADMIN-01, they require
immediate investigation:

  -  Administrative activity outside 08:00-18:00 CDT on weekdays.
     Robert does not perform scheduled overtime.  Unscheduled
     after-hours work is authorized only for declared incident
     response, which is logged separately in the IR ticketing
     system.

  -  Administrative activity on Saturday or Sunday.  MedDefense IT
     operations do not run a weekend shift.  Emergency weekend work
     requires an on-call rotation (Robert is primary) and must be
     paired with an entry in the on-call log.  No weekend entries
     exist for Q2 2026.

  -  Use of service accounts (svc_*) for interactive logon from
     any workstation.  Robert uses his named domain account for
     all administrative work.  Service accounts are automated.

  -  Administrative tool execution originating from any workstation
     other than WS-ADMIN-01 (or WS-ADMIN-02 during an authorized
     failover).  Robert does not administer from user workstations.

  -  Use of non-Sysinternals PsExec variants, non-signed WMI tools,
     or unsigned PowerShell script blocks.  Robert uses standard
     Microsoft-signed tooling exclusively.

  -  File drops or execution of tools in C:\Users\Public\,
     C:\Windows\Temp\ or C:\ProgramData\ in the course of
     administration.  Robert works from C:\Tools\ and
     C:\Scripts\ on WS-ADMIN-01.
