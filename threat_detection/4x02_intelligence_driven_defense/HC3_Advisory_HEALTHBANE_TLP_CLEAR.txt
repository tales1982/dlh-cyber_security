EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

HC3 is tracking a coordinated, multi-stage campaign -- designated HEALTHBANE
-- that has targeted at least 14 US healthcare organizations in the Midwest
ISAC region since 2026-04-14.  HC3 has direct or partner visibility on 6 of
those organizations.  At two of the 6, the campaign progressed through all
three observed stages (credential harvesting, malware delivery, data
exfiltration).  HC3 assesses with MODERATE CONFIDENCE that the operator is
a financially motivated mid-tier cybercrime actor.  Attribution to a named
group has NOT been confirmed; commercial tracking names circulating in
industry channels are noted but not endorsed by HC3.

The campaign's initial vector is spear-phishing email from lookalike
healthcare-themed domains, typically registered within 14 days of first
email activity.  Compromised credentials are used for lateral movement and
for delivery of a Stage 2 macro-enabled document that drops a Windows
executable.  In the two compromised environments HC3 has analyzed, Stage 3
exfiltrates patient and insurance records via DNS TXT-record tunneling to
attacker-controlled infrastructure.

This advisory is distributed TLP:CLEAR to the healthcare sector.  Recipients
are authorized and encouraged to share broadly.


1.  CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW
=====================

1.1  Campaign designation
    HEALTHBANE (HC3 designation, 2026-04-25).  No overlap with previously
    designated HC3 campaigns.  HC3 has not adopted the "VITALSCORE" label
    used by one commercial CTI provider.

1.2  First reported activity
    2026-04-14 (earliest phishing email observed at a HC3 partner
    organization in the Midwest ISAC region).

1.3  Geographic scope
    US healthcare providers.  Strongest targeting signal in Midwest ISAC
    region.  Two organizations outside Midwest received phishing but no
    confirmed follow-on activity.

1.4  Sector scope
    Observed: hospital systems, outpatient clinics, medical billing
    services, regional insurance administrators.  Not observed: medical
    device manufacturers, pharmacies, public health departments.

1.5  Attribution
    Attribution is UNCONFIRMED.  Infrastructure fingerprints (Namecheap
    registrar + Hostinger/DigitalOcean/OVH hosting + PHPMailer 6.6.0
    sender) are consistent with a mid-tier cybercrime operator but do not
    match a previously named group with high confidence.


2.  OBSERVED STAGES
===================

Stage 1 - Credential Harvesting
--------------------------------
Observed at: all 6 HC3-visible organizations (100%)
Timeframe:   2026-04-14 through 2026-04-16 (primary window)
Method:      Spear-phishing emails from lookalike domains registered
             between 2026-04-05 and 2026-04-10.  Emails impersonated
             healthcare-adjacent senders (staff portal, insurance,
             HR benefits).  Landing pages collected username and
             password via a PHPMailer-served HTML form.  Credentials
             were posted to attacker-controlled VPS infrastructure.

Stage 2 - Malware Delivery via Macro Document
---------------------------------------------
Observed at: 2 of 6 HC3-visible organizations (33%)
Timeframe:   2026-04-16 through 2026-04-22
Method:      Stage 1 credentials used to authenticate to cloud email
             accounts.  Attacker sent follow-up emails from the
             compromised account to colleagues.  Follow-up email
             contained a .docm attachment (HEALTHBANE_S2_invoice.docm).
             The macro pulled a Windows executable (svchost_update.exe)
             from a secondary C2 domain.  The executable established
             persistence via a scheduled task named "HealthSync Update
             Service" and a Registry Run key.

Stage 3 - Data Exfiltration via DNS Tunneling
----------------------------------------------
Observed at: 2 of 6 HC3-visible organizations (33%)
Timeframe:   2026-04-23 through 2026-04-26
Method:      The RAT deployed in Stage 2 exfiltrated patient records
             and insurance claims data by encoding them in base32
             subdomain labels of DNS TXT-record queries to
             data-sync.healthbane-c2.net.  Observed query interval:
             10-15 seconds.  Observed label length: 44-60 characters.
             C2 responses also use TXT records containing base64-
             encoded command strings.

5.  DETECTION AND MITIGATION
============================

5.1  Email gateway
    - Block inbound mail from the 8 domains in section 3.1.
    - Strengthen DMARC enforcement (require alignment, not p=none).
    - Flag emails from newly registered domains (< 30 days) for
      manual review or warning banners.

5.2  Network
    - Block outbound connections to the 6 IPs in section 3.2.
    - Sinkhole or block DNS resolution for the 8 domains.
    - Deploy DNS query-length anomaly detection (alert when subdomain
      label > 40 characters with base32/base64 character sets).

5.3  Endpoint
    - Deploy the 5 file hashes in section 3.3 as EDR/AV blocklist.
    - Disable Office macros by default; require attestation for
      macro-enabled documents from external senders.
    - Alert on scheduled task creation with name containing "Sync",
      "Update" or "Service" by non-administrative users.
    - Alert on Registry Run-key additions outside installer context.

5.4  Identity
    - Require MFA on all VPN and remote-access entry points.
    - Enable geographic anomaly detection for successful authentication
      events (alert on logins from countries where the user has no
      prior history).

5.5  Threat hunting
    - Hunt for scheduled tasks named "HealthSync Update Service" or
      similar variants on all Windows endpoints.
    - Hunt for PowerShell execution with base64-encoded payloads >
      1024 characters.
    - Hunt for DNS queries to *.healthbane-c2.net and *.meddefense-
      portal.com (primary and subdomain levels).

7.  REPORTING AND COORDINATION
==============================

7.1  If your organization identifies HEALTHBANE activity:
     - Preserve logs (email gateway, DNS, EDR, proxy) for 90 days.
     - Report to HC3 via HC3@hhs.gov with the subject line
       "HEALTHBANE-YYYYMMDD".
     - Include: organization type, estimated timeframe, indicators
       observed, stage reached, current containment status.
     - HC3 will not attribute the report externally without your
       written authorization.

7.2  If your organization has intelligence that contradicts or
     extends this advisory:
     - Same reporting channel with subject line
       "HEALTHBANE-INTEL-YYYYMMDD".
     - HC3 will integrate confirmed new intelligence into the next
       version of this advisory.
