# Clinical Escalation Contacts

Use roles and extensions, not personal phone numbers.

## Downtime Declaration

| Role | Extension | Responsibility |
|---|---:|---|
| Administrator on Call | 7000 | Authorizes hospital-wide downtime declaration |
| Nursing Supervisor | 7100 | Coordinates nursing units |
| IT Incident Commander | 7200 | Confirms outage and recovery status |
| Clinical Applications Lead | 7300 | Confirms Epic/LIS status |

## Epic Escalation

1. Primary nurse
2. Charge nurse
3. Nursing supervisor
4. Attending physician
5. Administrator on Call

Medication concern escalation: nurse, charge nurse, pharmacist on duty extension 7400, attending physician.

## LIS Escalation

STAT lab order issue: ordering clinician, lab accessioning extension 7500, chemistry lead extension 7510, laboratory medical director extension 7520.

Critical value process: lab calls ordering unit, receiver performs read-back, lab documents receiver name/time/read-back, no answer in 5 minutes escalates to charge nurse, no answer in 10 minutes escalates to nursing supervisor.
