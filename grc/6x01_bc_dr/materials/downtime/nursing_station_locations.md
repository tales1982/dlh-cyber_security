# Nursing Station Downtime Locations

## ICU

- Downtime binder: ICU charge nurse desk, red binder labeled "Downtime Forms"
- Paper MAR packets: locked lower drawer under charge nurse workstation
- Allergy verification cards: wall pocket next to medication room door
- Escalation phone list: laminated card attached to downtime binder

## Emergency Department

- Downtime binder: ED charge station, cabinet 3, top shelf
- Paper triage forms: triage desk left drawer
- Lab requisition forms: specimen collection cart, second drawer
- Medication paper MAR forms: Pyxis room wall file

## Medical-Surgical Unit

- Downtime binder: central nursing station, blue binder rack
- Paper physician order sheets: unit clerk desk, right cabinet
- Paper MAR forms: medication room, shelf above barcode scanner docking station

## Laboratory

- Downtime binder: accessioning desk, yellow binder labeled "LIS Downtime"
- STAT requisition forms: accessioning desk, tray 1
- Critical value call log: chemistry bench clipboard
- Specimen rejection log: accessioning desk, tray 3
