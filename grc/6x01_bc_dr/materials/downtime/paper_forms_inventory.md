# Paper Forms Inventory

## Epic EHR Downtime Forms

| Form | Location | Used By | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper MAR | Nursing station downtime binder | Nurses | Medication administration documentation |
| Physician Order Sheet | Unit clerk desk | Physicians and clerks | New orders during downtime |
| Verbal Order Read-Back Form | Downtime binder | Nurses and physicians | Document verbal orders and read-back |
| Allergy Verification Card | Medication room wall pocket | Nurses and pharmacists | Manual allergy verification |
| Clinical Assessment Sheet | Downtime binder | Nurses | Vital signs and assessment |
| Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet | Unit clerk desk | Charge nurse and clerk | Tracks forms entered after restoration |

## LIS Downtime Forms

| Form | Location | Used By | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| STAT Lab Requisition | Lab accessioning tray 1 and ED specimen cart | Nurses physicians lab | Submit urgent lab order |
| Routine Lab Requisition | Lab accessioning tray 2 | Nurses physicians lab | Submit non-urgent order |
| Critical Value Call Log | Chemistry bench clipboard | Lab techs | Document critical value call and read-back |
| Manual Specimen Tracking Log | Accessioning desk | Lab accessioning | Track specimen receipt |
| Specimen Rejection Log | Accessioning tray 3 | Lab accessioning | Record unlabeled or mismatched specimens |

Minimum specimen identifiers: patient full name, MRN, date of birth, collection date/time, collector initials, test requested and ordering provider.
