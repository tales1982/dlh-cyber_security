## Joint Commission Relevance

Use Joint Commission emergency management expectations when discussing continuity of patient care, downtime communication, staff role clarity and documented emergency procedures.

## Audit Framing

A defensible DR package must show: what systems are critical, why they are critical, what recovery objectives apply, what backup design supports those objectives, what test evidence exists, and what remediation followed the test.
