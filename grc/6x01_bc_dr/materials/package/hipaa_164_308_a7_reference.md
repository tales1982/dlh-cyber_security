# HIPAA 164.308(a)(7) DR Program Reference

## 164.308(a)(7)(i): Contingency Plan

Policies and procedures for responding to emergencies or other occurrences that damage systems containing ePHI.

## 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(A): Data Backup Plan

Procedures to create and maintain retrievable exact copies of ePHI.

## 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(B): Disaster Recovery Plan

Procedures to restore loss of data.

## 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(C): Emergency Mode Operation Plan

Procedures to continue critical business processes for protection of ePHI during emergency mode.

## 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(D): Testing and Revision Procedures

Periodic testing and revision of contingency plans.

## 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(E): Applications and Data Criticality Analysis

Assessment of relative criticality of specific applications and data.

## Evidence Chain

BIA → backup architecture → runbooks → downtime procedures → recovery test record → gap analysis → master DR plan.
