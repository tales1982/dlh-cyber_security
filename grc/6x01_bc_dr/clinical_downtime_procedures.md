# Clinical Downtime Procedures

**Version:** 1.0
**Prepared by:** Tales Lima, IT Security, with Nursing and Laboratory leadership
**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Use:** print both pages and keep them in every unit downtime binder

## Epic EHR Downtime Procedures

**Use this page when Epic cannot be opened or stops responding on more than one computer.**

### Who declares downtime (Epic)

1. **The charge nurse calls the Nursing Supervisor at extension 7100** as soon as Epic does not work on two computers.
2. The IT Incident Commander (extension 7200) confirms the problem. **The Administrator on Call (extension 7000) declares hospital-wide downtime.** Until then, act as if it is downtime: patient care never waits for the declaration.

### Where the paper forms are (Epic)

| Unit | Downtime binder | Medication record (paper MAR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICU | Charge nurse desk, red binder "Downtime Forms" | Locked lower drawer under the charge nurse desk |
| Emergency Department | Charge station, cabinet 3, top shelf | Pyxis room wall file |
| Medical-Surgical | Central nursing station, blue binder rack | Medication room, shelf above the scanner dock |

Physician order sheets and the Reconciliation Cover Sheet are at the unit clerk desk. Allergy verification cards are in the wall pocket next to the medication room door.

### What to do (Epic)

1. **Open the downtime binder and start a paper MAR for every patient now**, using the last printed medication list.
2. **Check allergies on the allergy verification card before giving any medication.** Ask the patient as well whenever possible.
3. Physicians write new orders on the Physician Order Sheet. **Verbal orders are allowed only with read-back, written on the Verbal Order Read-Back Form.**
4. Record vital signs and assessments on the Clinical Assessment Sheet at the normal times.
5. Write the time on every paper entry. Put the patient's full name and medical record number on every sheet.
6. Send lab and imaging requests on paper requisitions and phone urgent ones.

### Escalation (Epic)

Follow this order: **primary nurse, then charge nurse, then Nursing Supervisor (7100), then attending physician, then Administrator on Call (7000).** For any medication concern, call the **pharmacist on duty at extension 7400** before giving the dose, then the attending physician.

### When Epic comes back (Epic)

1. **Do not throw away any paper.** Wait for the Nursing Supervisor to announce that Epic is back.
2. The charge nurse lists every paper form on the Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet.
3. The unit clerk and nurses enter, for each patient, medications given first, then new orders, then vital signs and assessments, using the time written on paper, not the entry time.
4. **A second nurse checks every medication entry against the paper MAR.**
5. The charge nurse signs the cover sheet and sends it with the paper forms to Health Information Management.

## Laboratory Information System Downtime Procedures

**Use this page when the laboratory computer system cannot receive orders, track specimens or send results.**

### Who declares downtime (Lab)

1. **The lab accessioning desk calls the IT Incident Commander at extension 7200** and the chemistry lead at extension 7510.
2. The Clinical Applications Lead (extension 7300) confirms the problem. **The Administrator on Call (extension 7000) declares downtime**, and the Nursing Supervisor (7100) informs all units.

### Where the paper forms are (Lab)

| Form | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Lab downtime binder | Accessioning desk, yellow binder "LIS Downtime" |
| STAT requisition | Accessioning desk tray 1; ED specimen collection cart, second drawer |
| Routine requisition | Accessioning desk tray 2 |
| Critical Value Call Log | Chemistry bench clipboard |
| Manual Specimen Tracking Log | Accessioning desk |
| Specimen Rejection Log | Accessioning desk tray 3 |

### What to do (Lab)

1. Nurses send every order on a paper requisition. **Phone STAT orders to accessioning at extension 7500.**
2. **Every specimen label must show: patient full name, medical record number, date of birth, collection date and time, collector initials, test requested and ordering provider.**
3. **Reject any specimen with a missing or mismatched label.** Write it in the Specimen Rejection Log and ask for a new sample.
4. Accessioning writes every received specimen in the Manual Specimen Tracking Log.
5. **Critical values are always phoned to the unit. The person receiving the call reads the value back.** The lab writes the receiver's name, the time and the read-back in the Critical Value Call Log.
6. **If nobody answers within 5 minutes, call the charge nurse. After 10 minutes, call the Nursing Supervisor (7100).**

### Escalation (Lab)

For STAT problems: ordering clinician, then accessioning (7500), then chemistry lead (7510), then laboratory medical director (7520).

### When the laboratory system comes back (Lab)

1. **Do not throw away any paper.** Wait for the Clinical Applications Lead (7300) to announce that the laboratory system is back.
2. **The accessioning lead collects all paper** from the lab: every requisition, the Manual Specimen Tracking Log, the Specimen Rejection Log and the Critical Value Call Log. Units send their paper requisition copies to accessioning with the next runner.
3. Technologists enter every result from the downtime period, oldest first, using the original collection time.
4. **Results produced during the outage may be missing from the restored system.** Re-enter them from the paper logs; never assume they are already there.
5. A second technologist checks each entry against the paper requisition.
6. **The chemistry lead checks that every critical result in the system matches the Critical Value Call Log**, then signs and dates each log as reconciled.
7. **Paper handoff:** the chemistry lead hands the signed logs and requisitions to the laboratory medical director (7520) for review. The laboratory medical director signs off and sends the bundle to Health Information Management, which keeps it with the patient records. The chemistry lead tells the Nursing Supervisor (7100) when reconciliation is complete.
