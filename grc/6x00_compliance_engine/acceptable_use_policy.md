## 1. Purpose

This policy protects MedDefense patients, their health information and the systems used to care for them. It sets the rules every user must follow so that patient data is only seen and used by the people who need it for patient care or MedDefense business.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every person who has a MedDefense account, badge-based system access, or a connection to a MedDefense network, at every MedDefense site and when working remotely:

- MedDefense employees: clinical staff (physicians, nurses, pharmacists, technicians), administrative staff and IT staff.
- Contractors and consultants working for MedDefense.
- Temporary and agency clinical staff, including travel nurses, locum physicians and float pool staff supplied by staffing agencies.
- Students, residents and volunteers given access to a MedDefense system.
- Vendor and supplier personnel with remote or on-site access to MedDefense systems, including support engineers for clinical applications, medical devices and cloud services.

It covers all MedDefense systems and data: workstations, laptops, mobile devices issued by MedDefense, clinical applications (Epic EHR, LIS, PACS/RIS, pharmacy systems), email, file shares, networks, Wi-Fi, VPN, cloud services, and any personal device that has been authorized to connect to MedDefense resources.

## 3. Acceptable Use

1. MedDefense systems, accounts and data are provided only for MedDefense business purposes and for authorized clinical care. Any other use is not permitted.
2. You may access only the systems and data your role requires. Having technical access to something does not mean you are allowed to use it.
3. Your account is personal to you. You are responsible for every action taken with it.
4. Lock your screen (Windows key + L) every time you step away from a workstation, even for a moment.
5. Store MedDefense data only on MedDefense systems: the EHR, approved file shares and MedDefense-managed cloud storage.
6. MedDefense monitors and logs the use of its systems, networks and accounts to protect patients and data. You have no expectation of privacy when using MedDefense systems.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following are not allowed, without exception:

1. **Looking at patient records you do not need for care.** Do not open, search or print the record of any patient unless you are part of that patient's active care or have a documented business need (for example billing or quality review assigned to you). This includes your own record, family members, coworkers, neighbours and public figures.
2. **Sharing your account.** Do not share your password, PIN, badge or MFA codes, and do not let anyone else work under your login, including on a shared workstation. Do not use anyone else's account.
3. **Installing unauthorized software.** Do not install, download or run any software, browser extension, script or tool on a MedDefense device unless IT Security has approved it through the IT service desk.
4. **Connecting personal devices to the clinical network.** Do not connect a personal phone, laptop, tablet, USB storage device or any other personal equipment to a MedDefense clinical network, wired port or workstation without written authorization from IT Security. The guest Wi-Fi is the only network personal devices may use.
5. **Moving MedDefense data to personal or consumer services.** Do not send, upload or store MedDefense data using personal email, consumer cloud storage (for example personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud or OneDrive), text messages or messaging apps (for example WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram or Facebook Messenger).
6. **Going beyond your assigned access.** Do not try to reach systems, data, folders or network segments outside your role, including by guessing passwords, using another person's access, or using tools to scan or bypass security controls.
7. **Putting patient data into AI tools or outside websites.** Do not type, paste, upload or dictate patient information into any AI tool (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or transcription apps), translation website, file converter or other third-party platform unless IT Security has confirmed that the service is approved and covered by a signed Business Associate Agreement.
8. **Turning off security protections.** Do not disable, uninstall or work around antivirus, endpoint protection, logging, encryption, screen lock or VPN on any MedDefense device.
9. **Using MedDefense systems for illegal, harassing or offensive activity**, or for personal commercial activity.

If you are not sure whether something is allowed, ask IT Security before you do it.

## 5. Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

Use, view or share only the minimum patient information needed to do the task in front of you, and only with people who need it for that patient's care or for an assigned MedDefense business task. When you are done with patient information on paper or screen, close it, lock it or put it in a confidential shredding bin; never leave it where others can see it.

## 6. Remote Access

1. Remote access to MedDefense systems is allowed only for staff whose role requires it and who have been approved by their manager and IT Security.
2. Remote access must go through the MedDefense VPN with multi-factor authentication, from a MedDefense-issued device or a personal device that IT Security has enrolled and approved.
3. Do not access MedDefense systems from public or shared Wi-Fi (coffee shops, hotels, airports, hospital guest Wi-Fi) unless the MedDefense VPN is connected first.
4. Do not access MedDefense systems from shared or public computers (for example hotel business centres, library computers or a family computer that is not enrolled).
5. Make sure nobody can see your screen or hear patient details when working outside a MedDefense site.
6. Vendor remote access is granted per session, through the access method assigned by IT Security, and only for the system covered by that vendor's contract.

## 7. Reporting Obligations

Report **immediately on discovery**, and in any case before the end of your shift, any of the following:

- a suspicious email, text message or phone call (for example a request for your password, an unexpected attachment or an urgent payment request);
- unusual system behaviour (pop-ups, a very slow workstation, files that have changed name or cannot be opened, programs starting by themselves);
- a lost or stolen MedDefense device, badge or token, or a personal device enrolled for MedDefense access;
- patient information sent, shown or given to the wrong person, even by accident;
- anyone using another person's account, or anyone asking you to share yours.

How to report, both steps:

1. **Call the IT Security hotline at extension 7233 (SAFE)**, or +1 555 0100 7233 from outside the network. It is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
2. **Open an IT Security ticket** in the IT service desk portal under category "Security Incident". For suspicious email, also use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook.

Do not try to investigate, delete evidence or "fix" the problem yourself. You will not be disciplined for reporting a mistake in good faith; you may be disciplined for hiding one.

## 8. Consequences of Violation

Violations of this policy are handled through the MedDefense Human Resources disciplinary process. Depending on severity and intent, consequences range from retraining and a written warning up to and including termination of employment, or termination of the contract for contractors, agency staff and vendors. Access may be suspended immediately while a possible violation is investigated.

Some violations, such as viewing patient records without a care need or disclosing patient information, may also be violations of HIPAA. HIPAA violations can carry civil fines and criminal penalties for the individual involved, not only for MedDefense, and MedDefense may be required to report them to regulators.

## 9. Acknowledgment and Review

1. **New users** (employees, contractors, agency staff, students and vendor personnel) must read and sign this policy during onboarding. IT will not create or activate system access until the signed acknowledgment is recorded.
2. **All users** must re-read and re-sign this policy every year, at the same time as annual security awareness training. Access is suspended for users whose annual acknowledgment is more than 30 days overdue.
3. Managers and agency coordinators are responsible for making sure their staff and contractors complete the acknowledgment.
4. Signed acknowledgments are kept by Human Resources (employees) or IT Security (non-employees) for at least six years.
5. IT Security reviews this policy at least once a year and after any major security incident. Changes require CISO approval.
