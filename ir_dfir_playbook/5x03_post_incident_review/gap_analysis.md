## Priority summary

- **GAP-001** (P1): The only early signal of the account takeover, the RU risky sign-in at 18:14:41Z, was suppressed by a conditional access exception; enforcing the risk policy would have blocked the attack 8.5 hours before the first alert.
- **GAP-004** (P1): VPN access from an unmanaged device in RU gave the attacker internal reach; a device certificate plus geo-block would have stopped the stolen credentials at the network edge.
- **GAP-006** (P1): MSBuild has no business on a clinical workstation and Temp should never run code; a WDAC block would have stopped `update.exe` and the MSBuild loader that started the entire on-host chain.
- **GAP-011** (P1): Credential and Kerberos ticket material taken from lsass enabled every later hop; LSA Protection and Credential Guard remove that material from reach.
- **GAP-013** (P1): Flat SMB between workstations and an open `LIS_REPORTS$` share turned one compromised workstation into access to patient PHI; segmentation limits blast radius to a single host.
- **GAP-014** (P1): Ticket reuse and 14 PHI reads were already in the logs but nobody was alerted; these two rules would have shown the PHI access in real time and triggered SEV1 earlier.
